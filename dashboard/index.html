Where the numbers come from

  • Pack cost for LFP and silicon-anode NMC follows Argonne BatPaC (2023 dollars). Other cost ranges come from DOE Vehicle Technologies Office roadmaps and Argonne and PNNL sodium-ion work.
  • Energy density and cycle life for sodium-ion, LMFP and solid-state use manufacturer claims (CATL, BYD, QuantumScape) and DOE targets. Claims are upper bounds, so use the per-chemistry sliders to haircut them.
  • India inputs: PLI-ACC (₹18,100 crore, 50 GWh, 60% domestic value addition), the ₹2,000/kWh subsidy cap, and a 20 to 30% cost premium for India-made batteries (IEEFA).
  • Assumed, not sourced: safety scores, LFP, NMC and LMFP cycle life, solid-state and Li-S 2026 cost, lithium exposure of pack cost, freight, exchange rate, and when each chemistry localises in India.
  • Unverified: customs duty on imported cells and the GST rate (tagged in the controls). Check CBIC and GST Council notices before relying on them.
Sources: Argonne BatPaC · DOE VTO roadmap · IEA Global EV Outlook 2026 · PNNL sodium-ion · PIB, PLI-ACC · IEEFA on PLI
